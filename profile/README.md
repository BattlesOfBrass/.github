# ![Battles of Brass](https://github.com/BattlesOfBrass/assets/blob/master/logo/BOB%20Logo%20very%20small.png?raw=true) **Battles of Brass**



**Battles of Brass** is a tactical strategy game heavily inspired by [*Spirits of Steel: A New Dawn*](https://gavgrub.itch.io/sosand) by [GavGrub](https://itch.io/profile/gavgrub). It is supposed to be a simple alternative to all the overcomplicated strategy games out there.

##  🎯 Our Goals/Roadmap

  * **A simple but engaging strategy game:** Mechanics should be easy to learn while leaving lots of strategic options for pro-players!
  * **Extensive scenarios:** You will be able to play multiple different scenarios during different time periods including WWI, WWII, the Cold War and the modern day!
  * **Multiplayer:** Multiplayer is planned to be eventually implemented so you can play with your friends!
